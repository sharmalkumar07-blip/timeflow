# 🚀 TimeFlow

## AI-Powered Employee Productivity and Time Management Platform

### About

TimeFlow helps employees improve productivity, manage tasks effectively, reduce stress, and achieve better work-life balance.

### Problem Statement

Many employees struggle with:

* Poor planning
* Procrastination
* Work stress
* Interruptions
* Workload management

### Features

* Task Management
* Goal Tracking
* Focus Timer
* Productivity Dashboard
* Analytics & Reports
* Stress Tracking

### Research Foundation

This project is inspired by my BBA research study on Time Management and Employee Efficiency.
